# 076 — Global chart titles

## Goal

Give the three Global activity charts concise titles that describe their time granularity.

## Requirements

- The heatmap title is `Activity Calendar` (`活动日历`).
- The 30-day daily histogram title is `Daily Activity` (`每日活动`).
- The hourly timeline title is `Hourly Activity` (`每小时活动`).
- Keep each chart's data range, selected metric, and behavior unchanged.

## Acceptance

- The three titles appear in the Global surface in English and Chinese.
- Other app languages continue to use the English catalog fallback.
